FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will this bot make things up like AI chatbots do?

No. Unlike generative LLMs, MindChat runs on a 100% deterministic rule engine. When a customer asks about an order or stock, it directly queries your store database. If a record doesn't exist, it reliably prompts for an order number or connects to your human team.

Do I need WhatsApp to use this?

No! WhatsApp is completely optional and excluded by default. MindChat focuses on keeping high-intent shoppers directly on your store with our high-speed, web-native embed widget to maximize checkout conversions.

Which platforms can my agent connect to?

Your agent connects natively to Shopify (via theme app embeds & webhooks), WordPress / WooCommerce, and any custom website (HTML, React, Vue, Next.js) using our single-line async script tag.

Instructions to plug your bot in:
Shopify
1
Open your Shopify Admin and navigate to Online Store → Themes.
2
Click the `...` (Actions) button next to your active theme → select Edit code.
3
Under Layout, open theme.liquid.
4
Paste this snippet right above the closing </body> tag:
Shopify theme.liquid
<!-- RuleMind Bot Integration for Shopify --> <script src="http://localhost:3000/widget.js" data-server="http://localhost:3000" data-source="Shopify Storefront" data-customer-email="{{ customer.email }}" data-customer-name="{{ customer.name }}" async></script>
5
Order & Inventory Database Access: The bot accesses orders and catalog inventory directly through Shopify webhooks (orders/create, inventory_levels/update) at https://mind-chat-323j.vercel.app/api/integrations/shopify/webhook.
1

3

functions.php
// RuleMind Bot Integration for WordPress / WooCommerce add_action('wp_footer', function() { ?> <script src="http://localhost:3000/widget.js" data-server="http://localhost:3000" data-source="WordPress / WooCommerce"></script> <?php });
4
WooCommerce Database Sync: Sync WooCommerce orders and products by setting your webhook URL in WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → Webhooks to https://mind-chat-323j.vercel.app/api/integrations/woocommerce/webhook.
